What you can ask for

  • A page someone can open

    Reads the web, keeps none of your data.

    A tide, a price, a public note. The link is the whole app. Your records stay out of it.

  • A place for your records

    Holds your data, talks only to what you approve.

    Invoices, a customer list, a note you want kept. A secret is left in App Settings. The app can read it once.

  • A check before you trust it

    Runs untrusted things sealed off, then reports back.

    Hand it a file. The result waits on Verify, and it never sits with your apps.
